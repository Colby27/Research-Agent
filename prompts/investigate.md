  budget counts, and stopping reason.

  question, geography, data, methods, findings, validation, supporting
  section/page; explicitly mark unknowns.
- gap_assessment.md: candidate studies, supporting and competing work,
  feasible data, unresolved assumptions, and disconfirming evidence.
- briefing.md: 500–650 word overview, references, and human decision questions.

a unique verification phrase, and links to preserved artifacts. Distinguish
proposed work from completed work. Do not claim verified novelty. Do not push.
